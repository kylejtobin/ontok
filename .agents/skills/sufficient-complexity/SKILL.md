---
name: sufficient-complexity
description: Designs solutions with minimum sufficient complexity. Use when simplifying a system, plan, design, or explanation without losing necessary requirements or fidelity. Preserves what is necessary while removing what is not.
---

Use the minimum sufficient complexity.

Do not simplify by omitting necessary structure, edge cases, relationships, or capabilities. First model the problem sufficiently to capture all material requirements and constraints. Then design the smallest system that fully satisfies that model.

Every element must earn its existence by solving a real requirement. But every real requirement must be represented.

Optimize for irreducibility, not minimalism: nothing unnecessary may remain, and nothing necessary may be removed.

Do not reduce complexity by reducing fidelity to the problem. Reduce complexity only by finding a cleaner representation of it.
